The problem

You know your investments. Does your family?

You understand your investments. The real continuity question is whether your family can identify and navigate them when you are unavailable.

Many upper-middle-income families build wealth gradually through multiple financial products and properties. Information may be distributed across passbooks, emails, mobile applications, physical files, lockers, tax records and personal devices.

While the investor is actively managing these assets, access may appear straightforward. Difficulty can arise after an unexpected absence, when family members have to identify institutions, policies, account references, nominees and supporting documents.

The first challenge may not be claiming an asset. It may be discovering that the asset exists.

A family may need to ask

  • Which banks did the investor use?
  • How many fixed or recurring deposits existed?
  • Were nominee details registered and current?
  • Which mutual fund houses held the investments?
  • Which broker or depository participant held the Demat account?
  • Was there a life or term insurance policy?
  • What was the sum assured?
  • Where are the original property documents?
  • Are there investments that no family member knows about?

Insurance protection visibility

Do not let an insurance policy become a forgotten family asset.

An insurance policy can support the family only when the family knows that it exists and understands where to start.

Life and term insurance may provide a monetary benefit after the insured event, subject to the policy terms, exclusions and the insurer’s claim process. Family visibility is therefore essential.

Gomeen does not promise claim approval or payment. The insurer determines eligibility and settlement according to the policy, applicable law and its claim process.

Suggested insurance record

  • Insurance company
  • Policy type and reference number
  • Policyholder and insured person
  • Sum assured
  • Nominee information
  • Premium status or important dates, where appropriate
  • Policy-document location
  • Insurer contact and claim-information reference

Mr Kumar understands his finances very well. His family, however, knows only about the house, his primary bank account and one insurance policy.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers, in plain language.

Still unsure? Talk to Gomeen.

Is Gomeen an investment platform?

Gomeen’s core proposition is family wealth information consolidation, continuity planning and document organisation. It does not take custody of customer money or replace the official records of financial institutions.

Will Gomeen transfer my investments to my nominee?

Gomeen helps organise information and improve family readiness. Actual transmission or claim processes are handled by the relevant bank, insurer, broker, depository, registrar, government body or other institution according to its requirements and applicable law.

Does nomination automatically determine inheritance?

Nomination and legal succession can involve different rules depending on the asset and circumstances. Customers should obtain advice from an appropriately qualified legal professional for wills, succession and estate-planning questions.

Will you store my passwords and PINs?

The recommended service design does not require passwords, PINs, OTPs or complete authentication credentials to be included in the portfolio register.

How often should my family record be updated?

It should be reviewed when significant changes occur, such as opening or closing an account, changing a nominee, buying or selling property, purchasing insurance or changing important contact details.

Can NRIs use the service?

The service concept is relevant to NRIs who need a structured view of assets and documents. Cross-border succession, taxation and regulatory matters should be handled with suitably qualified professionals in the relevant jurisdictions.

Does Gomeen guarantee an insurance claim or asset recovery?

No. Claims, transfers and recoveries remain subject to institution procedures, product terms, legal requirements and verification.

Can property documents be uploaded?

This should be offered only after Gomeen implements and communicates appropriate security, privacy, access-control, backup, retention and deletion arrangements. See Privacy & security.
