Scope statement

Gomeen Consultancy Services Limited provides financial-information consolidation, documentation and family wealth continuity support. Services involving investment advice, securities, taxation, legal succession, estate planning, insurance distribution or other regulated professional activities will be provided only where Gomeen is appropriately authorised or through suitably qualified and registered professionals, as applicable.

General disclaimer

Information provided through this website and related services is for general information, documentation and continuity-planning purposes. It does not constitute personalised investment, legal, tax or insurance advice. Gomeen does not guarantee the approval of a claim, recovery of an asset, transmission to a nominee, investment performance or any legal succession outcome. Customers should verify information with the relevant institution and obtain advice from appropriately qualified professionals.

What we do not claim

Gomeen does not claim any of the following:

  • Guaranteed transfer to nominees
  • Guaranteed insurance claim settlement
  • Complete protection from financial loss
  • Government-approved or regulator-approved status
  • Bank-grade or military-grade security
  • Legal or tax advice
