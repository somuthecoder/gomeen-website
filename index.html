About

Systems that make project money visible.

With 15+ years across engineering and IT project management, I design the information backbone that lets leadership see where budget, time and risk really stand — before surprises become overruns.

My work spans Oracle Primavera P6, Unifier, Aconex, Primavera Risk Analysis, Ares Prism, Active Risk Manager and MS Project Server — from requirements workshops and solution design through configuration, training, UAT and go-live, and on to long-term support.

I am equally at home in Agile and Waterfall delivery, and have a proven track record of setting up PMOs that stay adopted long after the engagement ends.
